Coupez Support

Coupez handles local cut detection, two-video comparison, and AAF/conform workflows for post-production pipelines.

  • If export fails, include the exact AAF/conform step and message.

For mixers, QC, and finishing

Glass Master Support

Glass Master covers loudness compliance, dialogue analysis, PDF reports, and video finishing queue workflows.

  • Tell us which preset/spec you selected (for example Netflix or Disney Plus 5.1).
  • Include loudness values shown by the app (Integrated, LRA, True Peak).
  • For export issues, include selected codec and output settings.

For critical delivery deadlines, mention your target platform and due date in the email subject.
