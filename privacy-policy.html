Privacy Policy

This policy explains how GogoLabs handles information when you use our website (gogolabs.fr) and our apps, including Coupez, Glass Master, Odile, and FeedBacks.

Version francaise: Politique de confidentialite (FR)

Effective Date

April 14, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We only collect data that is necessary to provide support and operate our services.

  • Contact data you send us by email, such as your email address and message content.
  • Technical data provided by platform services, such as App Store transaction and diagnostic metadata managed by Apple.
  • Website server logs from hosting providers for security and reliability purposes.

2. Data We Do Not Sell

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

3. How We Use Data

  • To respond to support requests.
  • To maintain app and website reliability and security.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Services

Our apps and website may rely on third-party platforms, including Apple App Store, GitHub Pages, and Notion support pages. Those services have their own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

We keep support communications for as long as needed to resolve requests and meet legal, accounting, or security obligations.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you.

Contact and Updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at contact@gogolabs.fr.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.
