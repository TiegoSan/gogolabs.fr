Software studio for post-production

macOS tools for post-production.

We design software for editors, mixers, and finishing teams where reliability, clarity, and execution speed matter on every delivery.

Principles

A small studio with a narrow brief and a clear bar.

Local-first by default

Media stays close to the workstation, processing stays predictable, and exports do not depend on fragile remote loops.

Built for operators

The software is shaped around concrete decisions in post-production: inspect, compare, validate, export.

Less noise, more signal

Fewer states, clearer outputs, and interfaces that reduce friction instead of adding more process.

Apps

Our apps.

Coupez !

Shot analysis to conform-ready output. Fast decisions, cleaner handoff.

Glass Master

Complete loudness and delivery control. Measure, verify, and deliver in one workspace.

Odile !

Selected Pro Tools tracks to clean music EDL. No manual cue-sheet rebuild.

FeedBacks !

Mix notes to timeline-ready markers. From text or clipboard to session, fast.

Focused macOS tools for post-production teams who want cleaner technical workflows.
